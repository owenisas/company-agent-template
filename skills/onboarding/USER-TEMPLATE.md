# USER.md template

Stable personal facts only. No secrets, no customer histories, no
research dumps, no legal matters. Writes require explicit approval.

```markdown
# USER.md

- Name: <FULL_NAME>
- Slug: <employee-a|employee-b|employee-c>
- Role: <ROLE>
- Email: <EMAIL@company-domain>
- Timezone: <TIMEZONE>
- Socials:
  - LinkedIn: <URL or declined>
  - X/Twitter: <HANDLE or declined>
  - GitHub: <HANDLE or declined>
  - Personal site: <URL or declined>
- Bio: <ONE SHORT PARAGRAPH>
- Interests: <COMMA-SEPARATED>
- Working style: <PREFERENCES>
- Created: <YYYY-MM-DD>
- Updated: <YYYY-MM-DD>
- Provenance:
  - internal: <fields the person stated>
  - external: <fields from public research; cite source + date>
```

Do not invent a field the person declined. Mark declined fields
`declined` rather than guessing.
